MD PeeK Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Overview

MD PeeK is designed to preview and edit Markdown and plain text files selected by the user on their device.

Data Collection

MD PeeK does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app does not require a user account and does not upload user documents to our servers.

Local Files

Files are opened only when selected by the user through the Files app or the system document picker. Edits are saved locally to the selected file when the user chooses to save.

Network Access

MD PeeK does not require network access for core Markdown preview or editing features.

Support Contact

If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, device model, iOS version, app version, and message content. This information is used only to respond to your request.
